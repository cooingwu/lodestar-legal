隐私政策

北辰 Lodestar（以下简称「本应用」）· 发布者 CooHub · 生效日期 2026-11-05 · 版本 v1.0（初始版）
最终用户许可协议（EULA）→

一、总则：不收集，不上传

本应用不收集、不上传、不出售任何用户数据。我们没有账号系统、没有广告、没有营销推送、没有分析 SDK、没有崩溃收集服务。应用的一切功能均在你自己的设备上完成，我们没有任何服务器。

本政策即《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》第十七条所称的个人信息处理规则：它公开提供、便于查阅和保存，向真实、准确、完整地告知我们的身份与联系方式、处理目的与方式、信息种类与保存期限，以及你行使权利的方式和程序。

二、我们处理哪些信息（全部仅在你的设备上）

以下数据仅在你的设备本地处理与存储，存储目录为 %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lodestar：

  • 桌面与文件信息：为在画布上显示图标，读取文件名、类型、缩略图与修改时间；仅在本地渲染与记忆布局。
  • 照片元数据：相册插件展示拍摄参数（EXIF）时在本地读取，不外发。
  • 壁纸：读取系统壁纸文件用作画布背景。
  • 剪贴板：仅在你执行复制、粘贴等对应功能，或已获你授权的插件访问剪贴板时读取，仅在本地即时使用，不落日志、不上传。
  • 键盘与鼠标输入：为了让画布接收按键与拖动，应用安装了系统级键盘与鼠标钩子。按键仅在你使用画布时被实时转发到画布，不记录、不存储任何按键内容；鼠标钩子用于拖动协议与「桌面点击守护」诊断。
  • 应用设置与插件数据：全部保存在上述本地目录，数据由你全权控制。

位置（可选，属敏感个人信息）

星图「实时星象」功能需要经纬度做本地天文计算（恒星时）。应用启动时会尝试通过 Windows 系统定位读取一次位置；该访问完全受系统定位权限控制——你在系统设置中关闭定位或拒绝本应用的位置权限后，应用静默回退为按系统时区估算经度，星图功能不中断。

  • 用途与方式：仅用于本设备上的天文计算，经纬度（保留 4 位小数）缓存在本地数据库；绝不发送到任何服务器，不用于识别、画像或营销。
  • 拒绝方式：系统设置 → 隐私和安全性 → 位置，随时关闭；关闭不影响其他功能。

本地诊断日志

应用在本地目录 %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lodestar\logs 写入诊断日志（app-*.log 会话日志、perf-*.log 性能采样、input-guard.log 桌面点击守护）。日志可能包含上述本地处理信息的痕迹（例如文件路径、定位成功记录）。这些日志只存在于你的设备上，永不上传；历史日志会在启动时与运行期间自动清理（当前会话文件与守护日志保留）。

系统信息只读访问

应用还会以只读方式访问：注册表中的主题设置（跟随系统明暗）、文件关联信息（显示图标）、回收站条目（撤销「删除」时原样恢复文件）。均为本地操作，不修改系统安全设置。

三、保存期限

本应用的数据全部存储于你的设备，处理目的由你持续使用而维系：保存期限即你保留这些数据的期限。你删除对应数据或卸载应用时，处理即告终止。日志按上文所述的自动清理周期仅保留必要时长。

四、网络活动

本应用仅在以下情形与外部通信，且均由微软组件执行或由你主动触发：

  • 商店授权校验：试用与购买状态由 Microsoft Store 体系验证（Windows.Services.Store），相关数据处理适用微软隐私政策。
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 运行时：本应用基于该系统组件渲染，其自身行为由微软管辖；运行时缺失时，应用会引导你前往微软官方页面下载。
  • 升级跳转：点击「前往商店」时打开微软商店页面。
  • 插件市场：打开设置「插件」页或安装/更新插件时，应用从官方市场索引（GitHub 及其 CDN 镜像 jsDelivr）下载插件目录与安装包，并检查官方安全封禁清单。这些请求是普通 HTTPS 下载，不携带任何个人信息、账号或设备标识；下载的安装包经 SHA-256 校验与解包安全检查。网络不可用时仅市场不可用，其余功能不受影响。

除此之外，本应用自身不发起任何网络请求。本应用不发送任何商业性信息。

五、第三方插件

本应用支持第三方插件。市场插件上架前经审核（人工与自动化手段结合，具体方式可能随规模演进），上架与下架决定均不会仅由自动化方式作出；安装时向用户明示作者与所申请的权限（可能包括网络访问、剪贴板读写等）；确认违规的插件会被官方下架封禁，已安装的将自动停用并隔离（插件数据保留在本地、不会被删除）。

插件自身的网络访问与数据处理行为，由插件自身及其作者的隐私政策管辖，不属于本政策范围。请在意隐私的你在安装前阅读插件说明与权限申请，拒绝授权即不安装。

六、你的权利与行使方式

你的数据只在你自己的设备上，因此下列权利你都可以直接、即时地行使，无需向我们提交任何请求：

  • 知情与查阅：设置页可查看存储占用；数据均为本地明文文件与数据库，可直接查看。
  • 复制与转移：直接复制本地目录中的文件即可。
  • 删除：删除 %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lodestar 即彻底、不可逆地清除本应用的全部数据。
  • 撤回同意：关闭系统定位权限即撤回对位置处理的同意；停用或卸载插件即撤回对其授权。

（对应《个人信息保护法》第四十四条至第四十七条：知情权、决定权、限制或拒绝处理、请求删除。）

七、未成年人

本应用不面向不满十四周岁的未成年人，也不在明知其为不满十四周岁未成年人的情况下处理其个人信息；如需处理，应当取得其父母或其他监护人的同意。监护人可随时要求更正、删除相关数据——由于数据仅存于本机，直接删除本地目录即可。

八、数据安全

你的数据不出设备，其安全由你设备的系统安全机制（账户、磁盘加密等）保护。市场安装包经强制 SHA-256 校验、解包路径检查与清单校验后才可安装。我们建议你自行做好常规备份。

Privacy Policy (English)

Lodestar ("the app") · Publisher CooHub · Effective 2026-11-05 · v1.0 (initial release)

1. In short: nothing collected, nothing uploaded

We collect, upload, and sell no user data. There is no account system, no advertising, no marketing push, no analytics SDK, and no crash collection. Everything happens on your device; we run no servers. This policy is our personal-information processing rule under Article 17 of the PRC Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL): it is published, easy to consult and keep, and truthfully states who we are, what we process, why, for how long, and how you can exercise your rights.

2. What we process (on your device only)

All of the following is processed and stored locally under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lodestar:

  • Files & desktop: file names, types, thumbnails and timestamps are read locally to render icons and remember layout.
  • Photo metadata (EXIF): read locally for the gallery caption; never transmitted.
  • Wallpaper: the system wallpaper file is read to paint the canvas background.
  • Clipboard: read only when you use copy/paste features or an authorized plugin accesses the clipboard; used locally on the spot, never logged or uploaded.
  • Keyboard & mouse input: the app installs system-level hooks so the canvas receives keys and dragging. Keystrokes are forwarded in real time while you use the canvas and are never recorded or stored; the mouse hook serves the drag protocol and a desktop-click diagnostic guard.
  • Settings & plugin data: stored locally, fully under your control.

Location (optional; sensitive personal information)

The real-time star chart needs your coordinates for on-device astronomy. At startup the app tries one read via Windows system location; this is entirely governed by the OS location permission — if you turn location off or deny the app, it silently falls back to a time-zone estimate and nothing breaks.

  • Purpose & method: on-device astronomy only; coordinates (4 decimal places) are cached in the local database and never sent anywhere, never used for identification, profiling or marketing.
  • Refusal: Settings → Privacy & security → Location, anytime; other features are unaffected.

Local diagnostic logs

The app writes local logs under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lodestar\logs (app-*.log session log, perf-*.log performance sampling, input-guard.log desktop-click guard). Logs may contain traces of the locally processed data above (e.g. file paths, a successful location fix). They stay on your device and are never uploaded; historical logs are cleaned automatically at startup and periodically (current-session and guard logs are kept).

Read-only system access

The app also reads, read-only: registry theme settings (follow-system light/dark), file associations (to show icons), and Recycle Bin entries (to restore files on undo). All local.

3. Retention

All data lives on your device and exists for as long as you keep using the app: the retention period is however long you keep the data. Deleting the data or uninstalling ends processing. Logs are trimmed automatically to the minimum needed, as described above.

4. Network activity

  • Store licensing: trial/purchase state is verified through Microsoft Store (covered by Microsoft's privacy policy).
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime: a Microsoft system component used for rendering; if missing, the app points you to Microsoft's official download page.
  • Upgrade link: opens the Microsoft Store product page when you tap "Upgrade".
  • Plugin market: opening the settings "Plugins" page or installing/updating a plugin downloads the market index and packages from our official index (GitHub and its CDN mirror jsDelivr), and checks the official security blocklist. These are plain HTTPS downloads that carry no personal information, account, or device identifiers; packages are verified by SHA-256 and unpacked safely. When offline, only the market is unavailable.

Beyond this the app makes no network requests and sends no commercial messages.

5. Third-party plugins

Market plugins are reviewed before listing (combining human and automated means, evolving with scale), and listing or delisting decisions are never made solely by automated means; the app shows their author and requested permissions (possibly including network access and clipboard) at install time; plugins found in violation are delisted and blocked, and installed copies are automatically disabled and quarantined (local plugin data is kept, never deleted).

A plugin's own network access and data handling are governed by the plugin and its author's privacy policy, not this one. Please read the plugin description and its permission requests before installing; declining means not installing.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

Your data lives only on your device, so every right below can be exercised directly and immediately, without any request to us:

  • Access: storage usage is shown on the settings page; all data is plain local files and a database you can open.
  • Copy & portability: just copy the files.
  • Deletion: delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lodestar to erase everything, completely and irreversibly.
  • Withdrawal of consent: turn off OS location to withdraw location consent; disable or uninstall a plugin to withdraw its authorization.

(Corresponding to PIPL Articles 44–47: right to know and decide, to restrict or refuse processing, and to request deletion.)

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly process a minor's personal information without consent from their parent or guardian. Guardians may have data corrected or deleted at any time — simply delete the local data.

8. Data security

Your data never leaves the device; its safety rests on your device's own security (account, disk encryption). Market packages pass mandatory SHA-256 verification, unpack path checks, and manifest validation before install. Regular backups are your friend.
